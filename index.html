The Gangetic River Dolphin

India's National Aquatic Animal thrives in the Vikramshila sanctuary. We document their complex sonar communication and river habitats.
Birds

The Sparrowhawk (Shikra)

The Eurasian Sparrowhawk (Accipiter nisus), a small raptor, was recorded for the first time in Bihar's Rajgir hills in December 2025, marking the location as a new stopover on an international migratory flyway.

The Mechanism

FROM LENS TO LAND

We use the lens to acquire the land. Your viewership directly funds the re-acquisition of the very Earth we film.

1. Create & Broadcast

Produce a passion project with NATBIOS. We broadcast your documentary, generating awareness and revenue.

2. Acquire & Restore

Profits fund the NATBIOS Earth Fund. We purchase degraded land and use rapid-growth ecology to rewild it.

3. Live in the Balance

On the edges of restored forests, we build zero-emission Eco-Villages to fund the next cycle.

For Filmmakers

Pledge a Project

Pitch your environmental documentary to NATBIOS. If greenlit, your budget goes toward restoring the land.

For Residents

Eco-Village Waitlist

Join the waitlist to secure a lease in our zero-pollution, restored native forests.

Sustainability

Restoration in Action
